How to use the Japan driving licence information library

Begin with the guide that matches your current situation, then follow the related document, cost or test links. Each article is written for practical preparation and links to primary or recognised sources where possible. Because licence-centre procedures can vary, use the guides to understand the process and confirm the final appointment, documents, fees and supported languages with your prefectural police authority.
